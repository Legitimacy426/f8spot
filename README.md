# f8 spot

**Accept international payments on a page that's yours.**

## What it does

One page with your brand on it. Sell something, invoice someone, raise for a cause or let people support you. They pay from anywhere, in any currency, and the money settles into your own account.

## Highlights

- Branded payment pages in minutes
- Sell, invoice, fundraise or accept support
- Multi-currency checkout
- Payouts straight to your own account

## Built with

Next.js · React · Convex · Better Auth · Paystack · Tailwind CSS · Recharts
